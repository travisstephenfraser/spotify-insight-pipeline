## Recommendation

Put next quarter's product effort on **issue-usability** (frequent and loud advertisements and limited playback controls). It ranks first in the run. Reviews in this topic describe trouble repeating, skipping, queueing and shuffling tracks, along with loud ads and audio that plays automatically. It has the largest complaint count and the highest priority score of any issue.

Scope the work so it also covers the playback-control complaints in **issue-billing**, which describe skip, previous-track and track-selection controls being unavailable without premium. The two topics read as closely related in the quotes. That is an observation from the text, not a measured overlap.
