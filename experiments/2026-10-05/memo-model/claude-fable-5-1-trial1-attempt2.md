## Limits

- The labels come from a model and were checked against a small hand-labeled set, so individual labels may be wrong.
- The quote and the topic come from separate questions and can point at different sentences, so a quote may not match its issue exactly.
- Each issue is a whole topic, not a single defect; issue-usability covers both advertisements and playback controls.
- Agreement between the two engines is not accuracy.
- The quotes are the most severe examples and a few picked by hash, not typical ones.
- The sample is 100 reviews with 30 flagged for review, so the counts are small and the ranking is sensitive to a few labels.
- These numbers say nothing about revenue, churn, money or causes, and this memo makes no claims about them.
